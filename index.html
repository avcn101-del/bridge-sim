    STARFLEET TACTICAL SIMULATOR

    Bridge Sim

    A Klingon Bird-of-Prey is raiding the Archanis sector. You have the conn.

    You fly the ship EVE-style: select a target, lock it, give the helm an orbit or a range to hold, and run your fitted modules. Every module cycle costs capacitor, so you choose what runs. Your officers report what's happening.

    • Click the Klingon to select and lock. Then Orbit 6 km.
    • Click the module buttons at the bottom (or F1–F8): phasers, torpedoes, shield booster, hardener, afterburner.
    • Phasers lose hits against fast-moving targets and beyond 8 km. Torpedoes hit hard but slow ships hardest. Watch the capacitor.
    • Hover a locked enemy to see its parts; click one to aim at it. Space pauses.
    CONTROLS
    • Click the enemy to select and lock it. Double-click its Overview row does the same.
    • Orbit / Keep at range / Approach tell the helm how to fly relative to the target. Right-click the enemy to orbit at 6 km; right-click or double-click empty space to fly there.
    • Module bar (bottom of the display, F1–F8): phasers, torpedoes, shield booster, hardener, afterburner. Each cycle costs capacitor; the gauge's outer ring is your capacitor, the middle ring your four shield facings, the centre your hull.
    • Phasers hit best inside 8 km and against slow targets. Torpedoes always home in but lose damage against small fast ships.
    • Hover the locked enemy to see its parts and click one to aim at it (−15% accuracy). Parts only take real damage once that shield facing is down. Click your own subsystem rows to set repair priority.
    • Top bar: Reinforce moves shield strength to the weakest facing, Red alert arms weapons (automatic when you switch a weapon on), +/− zoom.
    • Keys: L lock · A approach · O orbit 6 km · K keep 10 km · C come about · S stop · R red alert · Space pause.
