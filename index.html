    STARFLEET TACTICAL SIMULATOR

    Bridge Sim

    A Klingon Bird-of-Prey is raiding the Archanis sector. You have the conn.

    Build your starship block by block on a Trek-shaped hull: armor, phasers, disruptor cannons, pulse phasers, torpedo tubes, shield emitters, reactors, engines, sensors, a bridge and crew quarters. Where you put them decides what they protect and where they can fire. Then fly it EVE-style: lock, orbit, and run your weapons and systems on a capacitor budget. Credits from battles buy blocks and bigger hulls; skills train in real time; progress is saved in this browser.

    • Click the Klingon to select and lock. Then Orbit 6 km.
    • Click the module buttons at the bottom (or F1–F8): phasers, torpedoes, shield booster, hardener, afterburner.
    • Phasers lose hits against fast-moving targets and beyond 8 km. Torpedoes hit hard but slow ships hardest. Watch the capacitor.
    • Ships are built from blocks. Hover a locked enemy to see them; click one to aim at that type. Space pauses.
    CONTROLS
    • Click the enemy to select and lock it. Double-click its Overview row does the same.
    • Stance (1–4) tells the helm how to fight: Engage orbits inside phaser range and flips sides on its own when the other battery or a fresher shield would do better, Skirmish orbits tight on the afterburner so their guns cannot track you, Standoff holds torpedo range and kites, Disengage runs. Ranges come from the weapons you fitted. Right-click the enemy to engage; double-click empty space to fly there (helm on manual until you pick a stance).
    • Module bar (bottom of the display, F1–F8): phasers, torpedoes, shield booster, hardener, afterburner. Each cycle costs capacitor; the gauge's outer ring is your capacitor, the middle ring your four shield facings, the centre your hull.
    • Phasers hit best inside 8 km and against slow targets; disruptor cannons hit harder and through a plate of armor but inside 5 km and only against slow orbits; pulse phasers are light, fast and track anything inside 4 km. Torpedoes always home in but lose damage against small fast ships.
    • Hover the locked enemy to see its blocks; click one to aim at all blocks of that type (−15% accuracy). Shots hit the outer blocks first and armor stops a beam dead; blocks cut off from the bridge break away. A destroyed reactor explodes and takes its neighbours with it. A ship with no weapons and no engines left is disabled: the fight ends and the wreck pays salvage. Click your own block rows to set repair priority.
    • Top bar: Reinforce moves shield strength to the weakest facing, Red alert arms weapons (automatic when you switch a weapon on), +/− zoom.
    • Weapons fire only where the hull does not block them, so a saucer fights best with the enemy on its beam; the helm reverses the orbit when the other side has more guns to bring to bear.
    • Plays (5–0) are whole strategies the bridge runs in phases: Alpha strike closes cold, dumps everything with the governor off and breaks off to cool; Siege holds station at torpedo range with everything that reaches; Turtle holds a broadside with hardener and reinforce; Hunter goes for reactors then bare hull; Ambush cloaks in, decloaks for a 10 s strike that doubles damage through their shields, and slips away cloaked (needs a cloak); Hit & fade engages until a shield runs low, fades into a tight afterburner orbit until it recovers, then re-engages. The Helm row shows the phase. Any stance, Stop or fly-to cancels the play; you can still toggle modules by hand.
    • Shield bubbles: each emitter protects the blocks within 5 cells; the rings you see are that cover. Hull outside every ring takes hits unshielded. The EXPOSED chip aims at the enemy's bare hull; the hangar paints yours red.
    • Crew: every system needs hands (the Ship panel shows crew on hand / posts). The bridge gives 10, each crew quarters 8; short of crew, weapons and shields run slower, and blocks far from any quarters repair at half speed. Lose crew decks in battle and the whole ship slows down.
    • Cloak and ECM fields: a cloaking device covers 10 cells and a jammer 7. You can cloak only when every block is inside a cloak field (the hangar frames the rest in purple), and a jam breaks the enemy's lock only when your whole ship is inside the ECM field; otherwise it just steers their fire onto the blocks outside it (green frames).
    • Heat: the bar under the module bar. Every shot adds heat (phasers a little, torpedoes a lot); at the cap weapons stop, and a salvo that crosses it overloads everything for six seconds. The pale mark shows where your next full salvo would land. GOV (H) is the heat governor: on, your weapons hold fire above 85% and resume at 60%; off, you can alpha-strike into an overload.
    • Scan panel (bottom): once you hold a lock it shows the enemy's blocks and damage, shield bubbles and bare hull, cloak and ECM field gaps, its crew, power and heat, how many of its weapons bear on you, and what its torpedoes would do to you. The bridge log is the two-line ticker at the top of the display; LOG (G) opens the full log.
    • Attack side (B cycles): in Engage or Skirmish the helm can shadow one side of the enemy instead of circling: Blind finds the sector where the fewest of their weapons bear (the scan panel shows their fire per sector), Aft sits on their tail where the engines are, Port / Stbd pick a flank. A big ship turns too slowly to shake a small one off its blind side.
    • Reactors power a range of 15 cells: systems outside it run at half, so killing the reactors in one part of a ship browns out everything around them, and when the last reactor dies the warp core breaches: 8 seconds, then a 2 km blast. Get clear (Disengage, 4). Severed sections keep flying, burn their engines, blow their reactors and hit whatever they drift into.
    • Critical hits: a hit that leaves a system under half its hp may cripple it: a reactor breaches, an engine sends the ship tumbling with no helm for 3 s, a sensor drops the lock, a shield emitter bleeds its facing, a weapon mount jams, crew decks are lost, a cloak generator drops the cloak. Aimed fire at wounded systems is how a small ship guts a big one.
    • Keys: L lock · 1 engage · 2 skirmish · 3 standoff · 4 disengage · 5–0 plays · B attack side · S stop (manual) · R red alert · H heat governor · G log · Space pause.
